# T-0034 Design Decision Packet

## Proposed Outcome

Produce one coherent design package that makes product intent, engineering standards, controller continuity, neutral assurance, cumulative-drift control, and future fresh-context agent orchestration explicit and verifiable without enabling runtime behavior.

## Required Workstreams

1. Project Continuity Baseline and controlled evolution.
2. HANDOFF budgets, bootstrap checkpoint, reading tiers, archive/evidence indexes.
3. Controller generations, context admission, safe checkpoints, active transactions, emergency recovery, and successor semantic equivalence.
4. L0/L1/L2 data flow, scope containment, envelope contracts, and evidence fan-in.
5. Neutral Audit Charter and professional product, architecture, technology, API/protocol, coding, QA, security, performance, delivery, project, handoff, and continuity roles.
6. VerificationPlan, assurance profiles, deterministic checks, independent review, findings, repair convergence, PASS/blocking thresholds, and user-decision escalation.
7. Step, anchor, and goal drift; continuity acknowledgements; drift ledger; golden references; visual/API/architecture/code checks; correction and re-baseline rules.
8. Executable acceptance and downstream implementation/review/installation/activation boundaries.

## Required Outputs

- Project Continuity Contract/schema.
- Bootstrap/checkpoint, HANDOFF standard/budgets, successor-probe contract.
- Controller data-flow and transaction state machines.
- Versioned controller/agent interface schemas.
- Neutral Audit Charter, role overlays, AssuranceProfile, finding/verdict schemas.
- Continuity/drift role contracts and ledger.
- Verification, acceptance, blocking, convergence, recovery, and anti-self-loop rules.
- Machine-check catalog and tests for micro-drift, style change, authorization traps, controller rotation, in-flight work, oversized bootstrap, false PASS, and missing baselines.
- Reviewed downstream boundaries.

## Risks And Mitigations

- Governance could distract from product delivery: use small versioned schemas, risk-selected roles, HANDOFF budgets, and an anti-self-loop acceptance axis.
- Auditors could become subjective authority: require canonical IDs/hashes, evidence, BASELINE_MISSING, and no gate/baseline modification authority.
- Hierarchical summaries could distort information: use machine-derived fields, evidence references, scope-subset validation, and mandatory high-severity propagation.
- Context isolation could be confused with file/runtime isolation: keep controls separate.
- Drift scoring could create false precision: use multidimensional severity/trend/authorization/impact with evidence.

## Acceptance

- Every workstream maps to artifacts, acceptance, tests, and review roles.
- User authority and design/implementation/installation/activation/real-project separation are preserved.
- Fresh successor probes recover equivalent control semantics without full chat history.
- Drift checks previous state, approved baseline, and user goal.
- Engineering assurance covers coding standards, technology choices, interfaces/protocols, architecture, style, correctness, tests, security, performance, and delivery.
- Missing baselines block invention.
- Context and audit costs are bounded.
- Independent review is required before implementation consideration.

## Recovery

Separately authorized registration recovery may remove only new T-0034 registration files and restore pre-registration governance projections. It must not alter T-0033 evidence, the candidate, global scripts, or historical mismatches.
